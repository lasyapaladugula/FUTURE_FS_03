# Pitch: Sunny Roast Cafe — Live Project Overview

Purpose
- Create a polished, modern web presence for Sunny Roast Cafe that positions it as the neighborhood’s go-to retro café.
- Highlight the menu, pricing, opening hours, location, and customer experience in a mobile-friendly format.
- Give the owner a site that can be published instantly and used as the foundation for future online ordering or reservation features.

Why this matters
- Customers expect cafes to have a strong online presence with clear hours, a menu, and an easy way to contact the business.
- A professional site helps build trust, increases walk-ins, and supports local marketing.
- The design reinforces the café’s brand identity with a warm retro palette, custom logo, and tasteful photography.

Owner pitch script (30–45 seconds)
1. "I built Sunny Roast Cafe a complete website that feels like a real local business launch."
2. "It includes a featured menu, clear opening hours, contact/reservation form, Google Maps embed, and a branded homepage designed to convert visitors into customers."
3. "This site is ready to publish now, and I can help you deploy it on GitHub Pages or any hosting provider so your customers can find you instantly."

Key features included
- Responsive homepage with hero section, menu cards, testimonials, hours, and reservation contact.
- Branded logo and favicon plus a warm retro café color palette.
- Contact form ready for Formspree or backend message handling.
- Demo authentication flow with signup, login, and dashboard pages.
- Backend demo included in `backend/` for future customer data, signup, and contact handling.

Next steps for the owner
- Publish the site live and share the link on social media.
- Add daily specials, seasonal menu items, and a newsletter signup.
- Upgrade the backend later to handle reservations, customer messages, and online ordering.
